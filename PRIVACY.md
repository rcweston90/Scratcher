# Scratcher privacy policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Scratcher is a text pad that lives in your browser's side panel. This page explains what it does with your information.

## What Scratcher stores

- **The text in your pad**, which you type, paste, drop in, or send from a page with the right-click menu.
- **Where you left off**, such as the scroll position and cursor, so the pad reopens in the same place.

Both are kept in your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your device. They aren't synced to your account or other devices.

## What Scratcher doesn't do

- It doesn't send your text or any other data to the developer or to any server. It makes no network requests.
- It can't read the pages you visit. It only gets text you choose to send it, by selecting text and choosing Send to Scratcher.
- It doesn't use analytics, ads, tracking or cookies.
- It doesn't sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.

## Read aloud

Read aloud uses the speech voices your browser provides. Some browsers offer online voices that send the text being spoken to the browser maker's speech service. That's handled by your browser under its own privacy policy, not by Scratcher.

## Copy and paste

Copy puts your text on the clipboard only when you click Copy or use its shortcut. Scratcher only sees what's on your clipboard when you paste into the pad.

## Deleting your data

Click Clear to empty the pad. Removing Scratcher from your browser deletes everything it stored.

## Permissions

- **Side panel**: shows the pad beside the page you're on.
- **Storage**: saves your text on your device.
- **Context menus**: adds Send to Scratcher to the right-click menu for selected text.
